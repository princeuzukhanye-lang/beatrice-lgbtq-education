LGBTQ+ Flags

Flags are powerful symbols of identity, pride, and solidarity. Each flag represents a unique community within the LGBTQ+ spectrum, celebrating diversity and visibility.

Rainbow Flag

The Rainbow Flag is the most recognized symbol of LGBTQ+ pride, representing diversity and unity.

Transgender Flag

The Transgender Flag features blue, pink, and white stripes, symbolizing the transgender community.

Pansexual Flag

The Pansexual Flag uses pink, yellow, and blue stripes to represent attraction regardless of gender identity.
