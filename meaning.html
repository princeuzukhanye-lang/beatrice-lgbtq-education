What Does LGBTQ+ Mean?

The term LGBTQ+ represents a diverse community of identities and orientations. Each letter stands for a group of people whose experiences and identities deserve recognition, respect, and inclusion.

  • L – Lesbian: Women who are emotionally, romantically, or sexually attracted to other women.
  • G – Gay: Men who are emotionally, romantically, or sexually attracted to other men.
  • B – Bisexual: Individuals attracted to more than one gender.
  • T – Transgender: People whose gender identity differs from the sex assigned at birth.
  • Q – Queer/Questioning: A reclaimed term for non-heterosexual identities, or those exploring their orientation/gender.
  • P – Pansexual: Individuals attracted to people regardless of gender identity.
  • + – Represents all other identities beyond these letters, acknowledging the full spectrum of human diversity.
