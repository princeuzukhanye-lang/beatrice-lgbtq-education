Uzukhanye Mlonyeni (Leroy Luzuko)

Uzukhanye Mlonyeni, widely recognized on social media as Leroy Luzuko, is an accomplished software developer and entrepreneur based in Cape Town, Western Cape. At just 18 years old, he has distinguished himself as one of South Africa’s youngest innovators in the technology sector.

Driven by a passion for coding and digital transformation, Uzukhanye has earned multiple professional certificates in full-stack development, software engineering, and advanced coding practices. His technical expertise is matched by a creative vision for building impactful digital experiences that empower communities and foster inclusivity.

In pursuit of this vision, Uzukhanye founded Beatrice Technologies (Pty) Ltd, a software development company dedicated to delivering cutting-edge solutions that merge technical excellence with social responsibility. Through Beatrice Technologies, he continues to champion innovation, inclusivity, and the transformative power of technology.

As both a developer and a leader, Uzukhanye embodies resilience, creativity, and forward-thinking ambition. His work reflects a commitment not only to advancing technology but also to shaping narratives that celebrate diversity and authenticity.
